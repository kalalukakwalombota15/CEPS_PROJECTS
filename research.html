Research

Master's Thesis

Title: "Short-Term Electricity Load Forecasting Using Machine Learning and Hybrid Time-Series Models"

This thesis investigated data-driven models for short-term demand prediction, comparing baseline statistical approaches with modern machine learning methods for robust performance across seasonal and high-variability periods.

Research Interests

  • Load forecasting and demand response optimization
  • AI for resilient and sustainable energy infrastructure
  • Interpretable machine learning for policy-relevant decisions

Future PhD Direction

I plan to pursue a PhD focused on trustworthy forecasting pipelines for smart grids, integrating uncertainty quantification, model interpretability, and domain-informed learning for practical deployment in emerging energy systems.
