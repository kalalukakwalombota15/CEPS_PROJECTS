Research

Current Doctoral Direction

Dissertation topic: Machine-learning-assisted optimisation of energy prosumer systems. The focus is on predictive control strategies, prosumer flexibility, and data-informed operation of distributed energy assets in smart-grid contexts.

Master's Thesis Foundation

Experimental Investigation of Flow Fields Around a Vibrating Body Using Particle Image Velocimetry (MSc, Technical University of Liberec, 2025).

This work established a strong experimental base in fluid–structure interaction, vortex dynamics, and high-fidelity measurement workflows, which now informs my approach to physics-aware modelling in energy systems.

Research Interests

  • Machine-learning-assisted modelling and optimisation of distributed energy systems
  • Predictive control and flexibility management for energy prosumers
  • Integration of physics-based engineering analysis with data-driven methods
  • Smart-grid resilience, interpretability, and deployment-focused analytics
