Research Profile

Doctoral researcher in applied mechanics with a foundation in numerical simulation and experimental fluid dynamics. Current research targets machine-learning-assisted modelling and optimisation of distributed energy systems, with emphasis on predictive control, prosumer flexibility, and smart grid integration.

My work bridges physics-based engineering analysis with data-driven methods to support intelligent and flexible energy infrastructure.
